MotoGP, leaned all the way over.

The fastest motorcycles on Earth, the factories that build them and the riders who take them to the edge.

See how they work

What makes them so fast

A MotoGP bike weighs about as much as a large dog and makes more power than most sports cars.

Huge power

Engines of around 1,000cc push these bikes past 350 km/h on the longest straights.

Extreme lean

Riders drag their knees and elbows through corners at lean angles beyond 60 degrees.

Wings and aero

Winglets on the fairing press the front wheel down under acceleration and keep the bike stable.

A race weekend

Every Grand Prix follows the same three days.

Friday

Practice sessions let riders and engineers find the right setup for the track.

Saturday

Qualifying sets the grid, then a short sprint race awards half points.

Sunday

The full-distance Grand Prix decides the main points and the podium.
